Pregnancy Mom AI Assistant — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 12, 2026 · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

The short version

Pregnancy Mom AI Assistant keeps your pregnancy and postpartum record on your device. An optional account exists — only to back your record up and recover it on a new phone; the app works fully without one, and there is no password (sign-in is a link we email you). We show no advertising, do no cross-app tracking, and never sell data. Something leaves your phone only in the specific cases listed below, and only when you take the action that causes it: explaining a lab report you pick, generating an AI summary, sending an AI chat message, buying a subscription (handled by Apple and RevenueCat), turning on family sharing, or turning on the optional cloud backup.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your profile: a name or nickname you choose, your due date (or last-period or IVF transfer date used to calculate it), whether you are expecting multiples, whether you have marked the pregnancy high-risk, and — after birth — the birth date.
  • Your appointments, and any reminders you choose to set for them.
  • Your log: symptoms, medications, weight and blood-pressure entries, kick-count and contraction sessions, postpartum recovery check-ins, and pelvic floor sessions. Weight logging can be turned off in Settings; the app never prompts you to weigh yourself.
  • Report explanations you choose to keep (the explanation text only — see below), your hospital-bag checklist and birth plan, app settings such as units, and simple on-device counters for the free AI tastes described in the Terms.

All of this stays in the app's storage on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it. You can export everything at any time (Settings → Export) and erase everything at any time (Settings → "Delete all my data"). Export is always free.

Lab report explanations — your photo is never kept

When — and only when — you choose to explain a lab report, the photo or PDF you pick is sent over an encrypted connection to our explanation service, which uses an AI model to read it and write a plain-language explanation. Before you send it, the app asks you to cover your name, date of birth, medical record number, and address on the document. Our service does not keep your report after the explanation is written, and the photo itself is not saved in the app — only the explanation text you choose to keep, stored on your device like everything else. Report photos are never part of family sharing.

AI summaries and chat

The app can turn your own log into a plain-language summary of your recent patterns, and it includes an AI chat assistant you can ask questions about your log in plain language. These features use our server only when you tap to generate a summary or send a chat message:

  • A minimized, structured extract of your log is sent over HTTPS to our backend, which passes it to our AI provider solely to generate the summary or answer. The extract contains your current pregnancy week (or days since birth), entry types, times and short details from the last two weeks, counts by entry type, and your upcoming appointment labels. It contains no photos and no report images. When you chat, your question is sent along with the extract, solely so the assistant can answer it.
  • The extract and your question are used only to produce your summary or answer. They are not sold, not used for advertising, and not retained beyond processing.
  • If you never generate a summary or send a chat message, nothing is sent.
  • Summaries and chat answers are educational information drawn from your own records — they are not medical advice, and the app does not diagnose anything. See the Terms of Use.

Family sharing (optional, off until you turn it on)

If you choose to share with a partner or caregiver, the app creates a shared copy of part of your record on our server so their phone can show it. Sharing is off by default and starts only when you create a sharing circle or join one with an invite code:

  • What is shared: your profile (name/nickname, due date and the profile fields above), your appointments, your log entries, and app settings. Values only — lab report photos are never sent, and saved report explanations are not part of the shared copy.
  • Who can see it: only people who join with your circle's 8-character invite code and hold their own member token (the app generates one per member; we store only a cryptographic hash of it). Members can be caregivers, who can also add entries, or view-only members, who can read but never change anything. You can remove a member at any time.
  • How it ends: when a member leaves, their access ends. When the last member leaves, the server copy is deleted. Your on-device record is yours regardless — leaving a circle never deletes your own phone's record.
  • The shared copy exists solely so the people you invite can follow along. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not analyzed.

Optional account & cloud backup

You never need an account to use this app. If you choose to create one (Settings → Account & backup), it exists for three things: backing up your record, recovering it on a new phone, and carrying your Plus subscription with you:

  • What we collect for the account: your email address — only so we can email you a sign-in link. There is no password. Sign-in is handled for us by Supabase (our authentication and database provider, acting as our processor); the link opens the app and signs you in on that phone.
  • What the backup holds: your entire record exactly as described under "What the app stores on your device" — profile, log, appointments, settings, reminder setups, checklists and birth plan, and the saved report explanations you chose to keep. Report photos are never part of it — they are never uploaded anywhere. While you are signed in, the backup updates automatically, and restoring on a new phone is a single tap (if the phone and the cloud both hold data, the app asks which copy wins — it never overwrites silently).
  • Leaving: signing out pauses backups. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes the backup, your seats in any sharing circles, and the account itself from our servers. The record on your phone is a separate choice: deleting the account leaves it untouched unless you also choose to erase the phone's data.

If a pregnancy ends

The app includes a quiet "My pregnancy has ended" option. Using it cancels every scheduled reminder on your phone, stops all prompts, and switches the app to an archived, read-only state. Nothing is deleted and nothing is sent anywhere — your record stays on your device, exportable at any time, and you can resume tracking in Settings if you used the option by mistake. There is no marketing of any kind attached to this state, ever.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no cross-app tracking, and no data selling — ever.
  • No analytics SDKs. The only usage information we can see is the anonymous, aggregate statistics Apple provides to all developers (for example, total installs and crash counts); it is not linked to you.
  • No diagnosis. Pregnancy Mom AI Assistant is an organizational and educational tool; it is not a medical device, and we are not a healthcare provider. We do not claim HIPAA compliance; instead, the app is designed so that your record stays on your own device in the first place, and leaves it only in the cases listed above.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple through the App Store and managed for us by RevenueCat. We receive subscription status (which plan, whether it is active) — never your card details. Purchases are linked to your Apple ID by Apple and RevenueCat; if you create the optional account, RevenueCat also links the subscription to that account so it follows you when you sign in on a new phone.

Who this app is for

Pregnancy Mom AI Assistant is made for the pregnant or postpartum user herself and the adults she chooses to share with. It is not directed at children.

Your choices & contact

Your data lives on your device, under your control. You can export it at any time (Settings → Export — always free), erase everything on the phone with "Delete all my data" in the app's Settings, or by deleting the app itself. If you created the optional account, "Delete account" in the same Settings removes the cloud backup, your sharing-circle seats, and the account from our servers. Questions or requests: info@annizon.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
