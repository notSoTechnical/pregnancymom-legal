Effective: November 12, 2026 · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

What this app is — and is not

Pregnancy Mom AI Assistant is a personal record and education tool for pregnancy and postpartum recovery. It logs what you record, organizes it, explains lab reports in plain language, and — if you choose — shares your progress with a partner or caregiver. It does not diagnose anything, and it never will. It never says any food, medication, supplement, or activity is "safe" or "unsafe": the app logs medications; it does not advise on them. Verdicts belong to your OB, midwife, and pharmacist. A value outside the range printed on a lab report is described only as that — outside the lab's printed range, and a question to ask your provider. AI helps, your doctor decides.

Warning signs

This app is not an emergency service. Vaginal bleeding, a severe headache, vision changes, sudden severe swelling, fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher, leaking fluid, regular painful contractions before 37 weeks, severe abdominal pain, decreased fetal movement, chest pain, or fainting are reasons to contact your provider now or go to Labor & Delivery. After birth, heavy bleeding, fever, incision problems, or chest pain are reasons to contact your provider now. If you may act on thoughts of harming yourself, call 911 or 988 (US) now; Postpartum Support International's helpline is 1-800-944-4773. The app shows these warning signs before any other content wherever they apply, and this safety information is never paywalled.

Reference information

  • Week-by-week notes: general information about a typical pregnancy. Every pregnancy differs; your provider's scans and measurements are the measure of yours. If you are expecting twins or more, timing and sizes differ from the single-baby notes — your provider's plan governs.
  • Visit schedule: the appointment cadence and screening windows shown are the typical US schedule. Your provider may differ — and if your pregnancy is high-risk, your provider's own schedule is the one to follow.
  • Recovery stages: the postpartum stages describe typical recovery in general terms. They are not a clearance and not a prescription.
  • Exercise gates: pelvic floor and recovery exercise content is shown in stages, and the later stages ask you to confirm your provider has cleared you for exercise. Start only after that clearance. Stop and contact your provider if you have pain, heavy bleeding, pressure or bulging, or incision problems.
  • Report explanations: generated by an AI model from the report you provide and can be wrong or incomplete. Check anything that matters against the report itself and with your provider.

AI disclaimer

The app's AI features — report explanations, summaries, and chat answers — can be wrong or incomplete. They are educational information generated from your own records; they do not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition, and they are not medical advice. When a summary or chat answer touches a warning sign, the app says so plainly and points you to your provider first. Your own provider, who can examine you, is the authority on your care.

Family sharing

Sharing is optional and off until you turn it on. If you create a sharing circle, anyone who joins with your invite code can see the shared record described in the Privacy Policy; view-only members can read but never change it. Only share your code with people you trust — you can remove a member at any time, and leaving a circle never deletes the record on your own phone. Do not use sharing to follow someone else's pregnancy without their knowledge: join a circle only when its owner has invited you.

Optional account & cloud backup

The app works fully without an account. If you create one, signing in is by a link we email you — there is no password to choose or remember, so keep your email inbox itself secure. While you are signed in, your record backs up to our server as described in the Privacy Policy, and you can restore it on a new phone. Deleting the app, or erasing the phone's data, does not delete a cloud backup: to remove the backup, your sharing-circle seats, and the account itself, use Settings → Delete account. That deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Signing out pauses backups; the record on the phone is never touched by signing in, signing out, or deleting the account unless you separately choose to erase it.

If a pregnancy ends

If you use "My pregnancy has ended," the app cancels every reminder, stops all prompts, and moves your record into a quiet archived state. Nothing is deleted, nothing is sent anywhere, and no offer or promotion will ever be shown in that state. You can export your record at any time, delete it entirely, or resume pregnancy tracking from Settings if you tapped by mistake.
