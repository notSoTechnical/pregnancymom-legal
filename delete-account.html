Pregnancy Mom AI Assistant — Delete Your Account

Effective: November 14, 2026 · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

The short version

Pregnancy Mom AI Assistant works fully without an account — your record lives on your device. The account is optional: it exists only to back your record up to our server and restore it on a new phone. Sign-in is a link emailed to you; there is no password. If you created an account, you can delete it — and everything our servers hold for it — at any time, right in the app. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Pregnancy Mom AI Assistant and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  3. Read the explanation, then confirm with Yes, delete my account.

Your account is deleted on our servers right away. This removes:

  • Your cloud backup (the copy of your record stored for recovery).
  • Your family-sharing memberships — your seats in any sharing circles. The circles themselves and the other members' own data are not touched.
  • The account itself (your sign-in identity in our authentication system).

Deleting your account does not erase the record on this phone — that record is yours and stays under your control. To erase it too, use Settings → "Delete all my data", or delete the app.

Option 2 — Delete by email

Email info@annizon.com from the email address you used for sign-in, with the subject "Delete my Pregnancy Mom account". We will delete the same server data listed above and confirm by reply. If you still have the app installed, Option 1 is faster and proves the account is yours instantly.

If you never created an account

There is no account and no server copy of your record to delete. Your record exists only on your device: erase it with Settings → "Delete all my data" or by deleting the app. If you joined a family sharing circle, you can leave it from the Family screen in the app.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Plus subscription. Subscriptions are billed by the App Store or Google Play — cancel in that store's subscription settings to stop future charges. Deleting the account does remove the server record the subscription was attached to, so Plus access ends with the deletion.
